Privacy Policy

Effective [EFFECTIVE_DATE] · Operated by [LEGAL_NAME] · Contact [CONTACT_EMAIL]

Pull Up helps you discover live music. This policy explains what the Android app collects, where it goes, and how to delete it. We wrote it from what the app actually does. We do not sell your information, do not show ads, and do not use third-party analytics or tracking tools. We keep our own anonymous usage statistics, which you can switch off in Settings.

Summary

  • Browsing shows needs no account and sends nothing about you to us.
  • If you sign in to add or confirm community shows, we store your email address and the content you submit.
  • Your location, Going list, filters and reminders stay on your phone.
  • The app sends anonymous usage events (which screens and features are used) tied to a random id, not to you. Turn this off in Settings, Share anonymous usage data, and the events already sent are deleted.
  • If you send feedback, we store your message and, only if you enter one, your email.
  • You can delete your account inside the app or on the web: how.

Who we share data with

We do not sell personal information or share it for advertising. These services process data to run the app:

  • Supabase (supabase.com): database and sign-in for community features. Our project is hosted in the United States (us-west-2).
  • Google (Gemini API, free tier): receives recognised flyer text (never images) and venue page text to extract show details. See the flyer row above for the terms that apply.
  • Deezer: previews and artist photos (band names, IP address).
  • GitHub: hosts listing files and app downloads.
  • JamBase: source of show data and ticket links.
  • Our email sender for sign-in codes: [EMAIL_PROVIDER, e.g. Supabase's built-in sender or Resend].

We may disclose information if the law requires it or to protect people from harm.

How long we keep it

  • Account and community data: until you delete your account, or until we remove it for breaking the Terms.
  • Shows nobody confirmed: shown only to signed-in people until the date passes, and stored until you delete your account or we remove them.
  • Reports: kept while needed for moderation, and deleted with the accounts they involve.
  • Server logs kept by our hosting providers follow their own retention periods.
  • Flyer submissions: the recognised text is deleted 14 days after processing finishes; the resulting show, its status and the job record are kept up to 90 days (job record) and the show until the date passes or you or we remove it. Deleting your account deletes your flyer jobs and any show only you supplied.
  • Anonymous usage events: up to 14 months, or until you turn off sharing usage data in Settings.
  • Feedback: up to 24 months, or until you ask us to delete it.
  • On-device data: until you reset the app or uninstall it.

Security

Connections to our servers use HTTPS. Access to community data is restricted by database rules so people can read only what they are allowed to see. No system is perfectly secure.

Children

Pull Up is for people 18 and older. It is not directed to anyone under 18 and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe someone under 18 gave us data, email us and we will delete it.
