Flyers and links you share

When you share a flyer image into Pull Up, text is read from it on your phone and only that text, with email addresses, phone numbers and social handles removed, is sent to our servers. The image itself is not uploaded. The text is processed by an automated language model (Google's Gemini API, free tier) to find the event details. Do not share flyers that contain private information. If you share a link, the app fetches that single public page to read its preview image and caption. Shows made from flyers may be shown to other users, labelled by source, once checked automatically or confirmed by a second person.
