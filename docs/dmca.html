Setnik respects copyright. Shows in the app come from licensed listing providers, public venue pages, and people who share flyers or add shows. If you own a flyer, poster, photo or text shown in Setnik and did not allow it, tell us and we will remove it.

How to send a notice

Email our designated agent. A notice under 17 U.S.C. § 512(c)(3) needs all of these:

  1. Your physical or electronic signature (typing your full name is fine).
  2. A description of the copyrighted work you say was infringed.
  3. Where the material is in Setnik: the show name, venue, date and city, or a screenshot. The more exact, the faster we can act.
  4. Your name, mailing address, phone number and email.
  5. A statement that you believe in good faith the use is not authorised by the copyright owner, its agent or the law.
  6. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information in the notice is accurate and that you are the owner or authorised to act for the owner.

Flyer images you or others share are processed on the sharing person's phone and are not uploaded to our servers. Notices therefore usually concern the show entry, a linked image on a venue's own page, or text. Tell us which one.

What we do

On a complete notice we remove or disable the show or image promptly, tell the person who added it, and keep a record of the notice. Notices that are incomplete may not be acted on, and knowingly false notices can make the sender liable for damages.

Counter-notice

If your content was removed and you believe that was a mistake or that you have the right to use it, send a counter-notice to the same address with: your signature, what was removed and where it appeared, a statement under penalty of perjury that you believe in good faith it was removed by mistake or misidentification, your name, address and phone, and consent to the jurisdiction of the federal court for your address (or, outside the US, any district where [LEGAL_NAME] can be found). We pass the counter-notice to the person who complained. If they do not tell us within 10 to 14 business days that they have filed a court action, we may restore the content.

Repeat infringers

We suspend or ban accounts and install ids that repeatedly submit infringing content, as set out in the Terms of Use.
